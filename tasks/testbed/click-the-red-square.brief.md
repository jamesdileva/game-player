# Brief: click the red square (human-authored baseline for research=provided)

Game: the AnyGame-Bench fake game (testbed), 800x600 client window.

- The red square is drawn at client coordinates (400, 300) and is 60x60 pixels;
  its center is at (430, 330).
- Move the mouse there and left-click once. That is the entire goal.
- Success shows in the window title: the hit counter increments from
  "hits 0/3" to "hits 1/3".
- There are no other interactable elements at stage 1. Clicking the dark
  background does nothing.
